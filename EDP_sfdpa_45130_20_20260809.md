## 1. The case (all facts below are sourced to the interview transcripts on disk)

Late on **10 October 2020** (06:31Z on the 11th), SFPD received a **robbery / attempted
carjacking with a knife**. Officers **Nicholas Delgado #2415** and **Kyle Roach #2457**
responded; Delgado requested the call himself on low band. They found the man in the street.
Roach: *"About 40 feet later, he turned back around and that's when I could see he still had
the knife in his hand."* Delgado's account: *"He then ran towards me with a knife in his hand."*

Commands escalate over roughly forty seconds — "get on the ground", "put the knife down",
"drop your knife" — and at **T06:34:51Z** both officers fire. The man goes down in the middle
of the road.

Then the part that makes this a film rather than a clip: **for nearly three minutes nobody can
approach him.** He is down, he is not showing his hands, and — *"we still don't know where that
knife is."* The officers keep talking to him: *"Put your hands out so I can give you aid."*
*"We want to help you, but we can't do it until you put your hands out."* Someone says *"Nick,
try Spanish."* A shield is called up. An arrest team forms. Only then do they reach him — and
immediately begin aid: trauma kit, scissors, *"multiple gunshot wounds to the chest"*, *"no
pulse yet"*, *"alright, compressions."*

**The systemic fact, in the officers' own words.** Asked by IAD whether they had Tasers, Roach
answers: *"We don't carry Tasers and we don't have any other less than lethal force options that
we would have been able to employ at that moment."* Delgado, to the DA: *"My only option was my
firearm."* To DPA: *"I had no other alternative."* That is the spine — not a contradiction
between officers, but a **consistency across three interrogations that points past both of them**
at how the encounter was equipped.

**Naming:** the subject is not named in our materials — do not name him. Officers are named in
the public record and may be named on screen.
