## 1. The case (FSR-verified, page cites)

2021-03-25, ~16:55: at **Home Depot, 4555 S. Western Blvd**, a Loss Prevention Agent observes
the subject concealing merchandise; LPAs approach as he exits (FSR p4). The subject shoots —
an LPA and, in the pursuit that follows, **a police officer are shot**; officers responding to
the person-shot call are pointed after him by civilians (FSR p1, p4). He flees into the alleys
off W. 46th Place. Officers **Tobias Houston #10647** and **Adam Tapling #9489** attempt to
stop him as he enters the backyard of **2540 W. 46th Place** (FSR p1). The subject fires
toward officers; **Officer Jack Kwa #7726** enters the adjacent backyard at 2536 W. 46th Place
to get a view (FSR p1 — the audit's "chair/fence maneuver" flank). Repeated exchanges; the
final exchange at **~17:03:51** (FSR p6, Att. 18). The subject is struck, weapon recovered,
aid rendered; he is pronounced deceased (FSR p1). A firearm and knife were recovered.

**Findings (exact, for Act 7 only):** deadly force **within policy** (FSR p1 conclusion).
Sustained: Houston — failure to timely activate BWC + firearm-discharge notification (G03-06);
Kwa — firearm-discharge notification (FSR p2–3). The Superintendent's **non-concurrence
disputes ONLY the penalty** (recommended suspensions vs "Violation Noted") — CPD agreed all
three violations were sustained (Nonconcur.pdf). **Never present this as a dispute about the
shooting's justification.**

**Subject naming:** FSR redacts the subject's name in our copy. Do not name; publication gate
"independent status check" stands.
