## 1. The case

A planned tactical operation at a residence. The plan is given on tape — *"Do everything. Mark is
your shooter."* … *"Protect you. Shoot gas, throw bangs."* — then containment (*"let's put a gun
on this closed door, guys"*), commands (*"Hands up!"*), and a **K9 apprehension**: the dog is
released across the yard, takes the subject down, and deputies move in. Afterwards an injury is
located on tape — *"he's complaining about the left thigh right there where he's got the
abrasions"* — and the file contains a **Use of Force Report**.

A civilian voice inside the house says *"it's just me and him and our baby dog"*, which — if it
survives the audible pass — is a quietly devastating counterpoint to a police dog being sent in.
Verify it before building on it.

**This is a use-of-force accountability case, not a shooting.** No one is killed. Keep the stakes
proportionate: the story is a planned deployment of gas, diversionary devices and a police dog
against a barricaded person, and what the paperwork says about it afterwards.
