# Critical Incident Community Briefings

The Mesa Police Department provides Critical Incident Community Briefings (CIB) to gain the public's trust by providing transparency through presenting the facts of a critical incident as we know now. These videos do not provide conclusions whether the actions of the officers involved are within policy or consistent with the law. View information on our [Critical Incident Review](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Public-Safety/Mesa-Police/Community/Transparency-In-Policing/Critical-Incident-Review) and our Department [policies](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Public-Safety/Mesa-Police/About-Mesa-Police/Policy-Accreditation).

[CIB Videos](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Public-Safety/Mesa-Police/Community/Transparency-In-Policing/Community-Briefings#panel-1-1) [Explanation of Tools](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Public-Safety/Mesa-Police/Community/Transparency-In-Policing/Community-Briefings#panel-1-2)
